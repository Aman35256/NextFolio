# Recruiter Preferences Guide

## 1. What Recruiter Scans For (First 6 Seconds)
- **Job Titles**: Recruiters look for progression and title alignment matching the role.
- **Key Technologies**: Cleanly listed skills matching the job specifications.
- **Company Brands**: Recognized tech companies, startups, or agencies.
- **Duration**: Employment stability (avoiding frequent short-term hopping unless freelancing is specified).
- **Achievements**: Concrete metrics showing how you impacted business objectives.

## 2. Portfolios and Project Showcase
- **Working Links**: Broken GitHub or demo links are immediate red flags.
- **Readable Summaries**: A 2-line summary explaining the project's purpose and your role.
- **Tech Stack badges**: Clearly outline the technologies used.
- **Architecture explanation**: Include a brief system design diagram or rationale.

## 3. Communication & Tone
- **Professionalism**: Concise, polite, and confident writing style.
- **Active Voice**: Use active verbs ("I designed") rather than passive ("A design was created").
- **Clarity**: Avoid fluff, buzzwords, and jargon.
- **Consistency**: Keep spacing, bullet styling, and headings uniform.
