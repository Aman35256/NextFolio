# Resume Writing Guide

## 1. Structure and Layout
- Keep it clean, simple, and chronological.
- Use a single-column layout. Avoid complex multi-column grid layouts which break ATS reading flow.
- Use standard margins (0.5 to 1.0 inch) and standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Times New Roman).
- Font sizes: 10-12pt for body text, 14-16pt for section headings, 20-24pt for your name.

## 2. Key Sections
- **Contact Information**: Name, professional email, phone number, location (City, State), LinkedIn, and GitHub.
- **Professional Summary**: A 3-4 sentence pitch highlighting your experience level, core expertise, and value proposition.
- **Work Experience**: Reverse chronological order. Detail company name, role, duration (dates), and achievements.
- **Projects**: Showcase 2-4 impactful technical projects. Provide description, technologies used, and links.
- **Skills**: Group skills logically (e.g., Frontend, Backend, Databases, DevOps, Languages).
- **Education**: Degree, school, field of study, graduation year.
- **Certifications**: Relevant industry certifications.

## 3. Formatting Content
- Use bullet points, not paragraph blocks, for experience and projects.
- Start each bullet with a strong action verb (e.g. Developed, Led, Architected, Optimized).
- Quantify achievements whenever possible (e.g. "reduced latency by 30%", "increased conversion rate by 15%", "managed a team of 4 devs").
- Avoid generic lists of responsibilities. Focus on results and accomplishments.
